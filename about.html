Who is this guy?

I'm Neil Patel, a Computer Science student at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. I like building the real version of hard things instead of importing a library that already did them: a Raft engine instead of pulling in etcd, an HNSW index from scratch in Rust, induction-head probing with a hand-written Triton kernel, netcode with an honest 64Hz tick.

Most of my projects start with "can I actually build this myself?" and end as something you can clone and run. When I am not doing that, I am probably making a website look like it is from 1999 on purpose.
